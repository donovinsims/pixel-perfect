August & Co.

The art of mindful mornings.

Get Directions

August & Co. is a scandinavian room on 33 E 17th St, known for the art of mindful mornings. Regulars come for the artisan bakery counter and stay for the playlist that runs from open to close.

Whether you are grabbing a quick cortado or settling in with a book, August & Co. keeps things scandinavian and unhurried. Expect seasonal beans, a short food menu, and staff who remember your order.
