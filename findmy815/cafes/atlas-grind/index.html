Atlas Grind

Global coffee, local spirit.

Get Directions

Atlas Grind is a industrial room on 118 W 22nd St, known for global coffee, local spirit. Regulars come for the specialty coffee counter and stay for the playlist that runs from open to close.

Whether you are grabbing a quick cortado or settling in with a book, Atlas Grind keeps things industrial and unhurried. Expect seasonal beans, a short food menu, and staff who remember your order.
